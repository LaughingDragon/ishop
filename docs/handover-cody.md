*DRAFT for Liam's review.*

Hi Cody. The site is built and it's yours. This page says what runs by itself, the two things you'll need to renew now and then, and who to call.

**Your site:** https://ishoponamazonwaytoomuch.ohbah.com. Sign in as **theowner**. It remembers you, so you won't keep getting asked to log in.

## Starting fresh

The Stockroom is empty on purpose. You took your listings down so every item goes through the site from the start. For each item: **Add** it (photos first), let the site write and price the listing, post it from **Post this item**, and mark it **Sold here** when it sells. The site takes care of the rest.

## What runs by itself

- **Morning digest** at about 7 a.m.: what needs you today, bills due, your calendar, and what's waiting to be posted. You can turn it off on your account page.
- **Needs you now** on the front desk: anything waiting on you (sales to ship, listings to take down, stale listings, bills).
- **Backups every night**, plus daily copies of the whole server. You don't need to do anything.
- **Updates every Thursday night.** If an update goes wrong, it undoes itself and tells Liam.
- **Old photos and labels clean themselves up:** a sold item's photos and its shipping label (which has the buyer's address) are deleted 30 days after delivery.

## Two things to renew

**1. Your AI key, every 30 days.** The AI writing and pricing use your own Anthropic account. Its keys last 30 days. You'll get an email a week before, 2 days before and the day before it runs out. When you do:

1. Go to **console.anthropic.com** and sign in.
2. **Settings > API keys > Create key**. Name it "Reseller Matrix" plus the month.
3. Copy the key (Anthropic only shows it once).
4. On the site, open **AI setup**, paste it, and tap **Test and switch to this key**.

If you miss it, nothing breaks: the AI just pauses, and everything else keeps working until you add a new key.

**2. The Gmail app password, only if mail stops.** The site sends your mail through the business Gmail with an "app password" (16 letters from Google, not your Gmail password). If the front desk says mail stopped, or you change your Gmail password:

1. Go to **myaccount.google.com/apppasswords** in the business Gmail and make a new one.
2. On the site, open **Mail setup**. In the **top** part, paste the 16 letters and tap **Save and send a test email**.

The same password also lets the site read your marketplace emails once that's switched on, so you only ever need one.

## Summary

Birthday mode is built and waiting for Cody's date (punch item 136). On the birthday, all day (Pacific time), every Reseller Matrix page turns into the party: purple and gold, four party stripes instead of the bulbs, pink room tiles and a confetti sign on the dashboard. The first page Cody opens that day starts his song. On iPhone Safari that takes one tap on a full-screen sign, because Safari never plays sound before a tap. **Cody can't see any of it before the day:** not this report, not item 135 or 136, not the settings page, not a preview.

## What was done

- **Look** (item 135 colors): ground #1B1030, panels #261845, gold, pink, orange and lavender stripes. Motto "Reseller Matrix · birthday edition". Room icon tiles in pink with dark text (4.7:1 contrast).
- **Dashboard:** a gold-bordered sign with CSS confetti, the pink badge "Today is a very big deal", "Happy birthday, boss!" and the line from the design. A big gold **Start the party** button toggles to **Pause the party**. "Needs you now" says "Nothing that can't wait until tomorrow. It's your birthday." One difference from the design: if a real take-down is waiting (an item sold elsewhere), it still shows, so nothing gets sold twice.
- **First open on the day** (Liam's request): the first page each account opens on the birthday launches the party, once per account per day, on whichever device comes first. It plays at once where the browser allows. Otherwise a full-screen sign says "Tap anywhere to start the party", and the first tap starts the song. "Not now" closes it quietly.
- **Song:** moved from your home folder into private files (`private/birthday/`). It's not in git and can't be fetched publicly, and the copy in your home folder is gone (checksum matched first). It's served only to signed-in accounts that have the mode on. It loops. Other pages offer **Resume the party** and carry on from the same spot. CSP now allows media from the site itself.
- **Reduced motion:** no confetti movement, and the stripes don't twinkle.
- **Switches** at /site-office/birthday (webmaster and site-foreman only): the date, Automatic / On for everyone / Off even on the day, **Preview it for me only**, and **Play the first-open launch again for me**.
- **Hiding it from Cody** (Liam: "hide the birthday stuff from the owner"): a new **crew-only** flag hides reports, punch items and decisions from everyone but webmaster and site-foreman. Cody's administrator account can normally see everything, so the flag works on three levels: lists leave them out, opening one gives "Page not found", and a crew-only report never shows up as a phase's "Latest report". Items 135, 136 and this report are crew-only.
- **TEMPORARY.md** (new, in the repository) lists every birthday file and the steps to switch off, delete the song and remove the code after the day.

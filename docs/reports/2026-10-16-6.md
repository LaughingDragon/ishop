## Summary

Every sale now goes into a price book. When Cody looks at an item, the site first shows what his own similar sales fetched, for example "You sold 3 similar items for $8-$15 (Home & Kitchen, Good)." That line leads the **Check sold prices** box and **Why this price**, ahead of the AI's estimate. No sales are recorded yet, so the line appears after the first **Sold here** in a category.

## What was done

- When a listing is marked **Sold here**, it keeps the sold price plus the item's category and condition at that moment, so editing the item later doesn't change its entry. If the sale is un-marked, those details are cleared and the sale leaves the price book.
- "Similar" means the same site category. When at least two of those sales were also in the item's condition, only those count, and the condition is named.
- Shown in two places on the item page: the top of **Check sold prices**, and the first line of **Why this price**, where the AI's number is now labelled **AI estimate:**.
- Prices show without needless cents ($8, $12.50). One sale reads "You sold 1 similar item for $12.50".
- The Lower / About right / Higher buttons and the typed price (step A) are unchanged and still recorded.

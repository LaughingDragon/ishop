## Summary

My office now has a **Year-end tax export**: for each year, a **Sales** spreadsheet (CSV) and a **Donations** spreadsheet. Every sale has its price, item cost, fees, shipping and profit, and the donation log shows when each item was donated and Cody's estimate of its value. Nothing is guessed: anything missing stays blank and is named in a "Check" column.

## What was done

- **Sales CSV:** Date sold, Item, Platform, Sale price, Item cost, Platform fees, Shipping paid, Profit, Check, plus a TOTAL row. Profit is calculated exactly as on the Money page.
- **Donations CSV:** Date donated, Item, Category, Condition, Estimated value, Item cost, Check, plus a TOTAL row. The date comes from the item's history: the day it was first set to Donated.
- **New item field: Estimated donation value**, next to the status on the item's edit form, shown in its details. Only Cody fills it in: the site never estimates a tax figure (decision 178).
- **Plain numbers** (`12.50`) so spreadsheets can add them up. Files are named `ishop-2026-sales.csv` and `ishop-2026-donations.csv`.
- **Years offered:** every year that has sales or donations, plus the current one.
- **Phase 5:** mileage and expenses join this export when Phase 5 adds them.

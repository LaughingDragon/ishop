## Summary

eBay is now a selling platform everywhere on the site, like Poshmark and Mercari. Every item has an eBay row, and the three existing items (121, 255, 256) got theirs set to **To post**. When something sells on eBay, the site tells Cody to take it down everywhere else, and the reverse works too. This is step 1 of Liam's go (item 280). It comes first because it stops double-selling. The **Check sold prices** panel (step A of item 270) also went live just before this, and gets its own check by Cody (item 277).

## What was done

- **eBay listing row** on every item: status, link, date listed, sold for, fees and shipping, the same as the other platforms. New items get one automatically.
- **Sold here cascade**: a sale on eBay marks every live listing **Take it down** and the rest **Not needed (sold)**. Undo restores them.
- **Post this item > eBay**: title (80 characters max), description (4,000 max), category, brand, size, color, a condition guess, price and the photos. eBay's condition list changes with the category, so the condition is a best guess and the page says to pick from eBay's own dropdown. Clothing gets eBay's clothing conditions (for example "Pre-owned - Good"); everything else gets "New / Used / For parts or not working". The price note shows a rough take-home after eBay's 13.6% final value fee and the $0.30 or $0.40 per-order fee. Photos export at up to 1600 px, not squared. The text is checked against the old app's eBay formatter (fixture test).
- **Eligibility chip**: eBay takes almost everything. Used devices with lithium batteries get a note: ship by USPS Ground Advantage and choose hazmat on the label. Chemicals get a "check eBay's hazmat policy first" note.
- **Category hints**: each site category names its eBay match, for example Holiday & Seasonal > "Home & Garden > Holiday & Seasonal Décor".
- **Add item > already selling**: eBay links (ebay.com and ebay.us share links) are recognised.
- Stale nudges, Stockroom bulk actions, the Money page and the tax export include eBay automatically. They work from the same platform list.

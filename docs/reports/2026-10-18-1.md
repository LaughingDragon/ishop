## What was done

- **Phases renumbered** (Liam): 9 = shipping and marketplace mail (was 10), 10 = listings import (was 11),
  11 = close-out (was 9). The status board keeps each phase's status and reports. ROADMAP standing rule 9: the
  close-out is always last and has the highest number. Older reports, decisions and code comments still use the
  old numbers ("Phase 10 A" = 9 A).
- **Cody's file** moved off the home folder into private storage (outside the website, never in git). The old
  Crosslister records (112) and the 11 photos that survive were copied there too, for the fill-in option.
- **Import listings page**, in 4 steps:
  1. Upload the .xlsx or .csv as it is.
  2. Columns: the site picks the tab, guesses each column and remembers the choices for next time. Columns with
     buyer details (buyer, address, email, phone...) are never read.
  3. Categories: each "Type" and condition in the sheet is matched to the site's own, and Cody can change any.
  4. Check and import. A summary table shows the per-platform counts (Posted, Sold here, Take down, To post,
     Not needed, Not allowed, not in sheet) with "do these numbers match what you see live?". Every item is
     listed with what it becomes and any problems. Items with a warning are left unticked. Cody chooses what
     happens to sold items (sold and already delivered / sold and still to ship / leave out). There's also an
     option to fill in details from the old Crosslister records.
- **Import** creates one item per product, marked Cleared to sell, in Game room > Table unless the sheet says
  otherwise. Each platform listing gets exactly the status the sheet gives. Platforms the sheet doesn't have (eBay)
  are left **blank, not "To post"**. Sold items go through the normal Sold-here cascade. Sold prices and dates are
  never guessed: if one is missing, it is left blank and flagged. No marketplace page is fetched.
- **Undo**: a whole import can be undone for 7 days. It deletes only the items that import created (and their
  copied photos).
- Item pages without photos now say "No photos yet. Add photos when you can (Edit)."
- `drush ishop:import-preview <file>` shows the same preview in the terminal. It saves nothing.

## Summary

Every item now gets six listings, one each for Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Grailed, Vinted and Facebook. They're edited in a grid on the item page, which becomes stacked cards on a phone.

Marking one "Sold here" asks first and shows exactly what will change. It then takes down the other live listings, marks the unposted ones "Not needed - sold", and sets the item to Sold. Un-marking puts back exactly what the cascade changed, and nothing anyone changed since.

## What was done

- **Platform listing entity:** a small custom entity with its own revision history, as approved.
  - Fields: item, platform, status (the 7 statuses), listing URL, listed price, sold price, fees, **shipping paid by the seller** (review gap 5), date posted, and eligibility override and note.
  - **One listing per item per platform**, enforced by a validation constraint.
  - Listings can't be deleted by hand; they're deleted with their item.
  - Access through a new permission, "manage platform listings", given to site-foreman. Webmaster and Cody (administrator) have it already. Anonymous visitors: no access.
- **Six "To post" listings** are created with every new item.
- **Listings grid** on the item page:
  - status, URL and the four money fields per platform, with one Save button;
  - on phones, each platform is a card with labelled fields, the money fields two per row, and 44 px tap targets.
- **"Sold here" cascade** (review gap 6):
  - **Confirmation step:** "Mark sold on Mercari?" lists each change (for example "Poshmark: Posted → Take down") before anything happens. Other edits in the same save are kept; only the sale waits.
  - **Rules:** Posted and Posted - at risk become Take down; To post becomes Not needed - sold; Take down, Not allowed and Not needed are left alone. The item becomes **Sold**, and its previous status is remembered.
  - **Undo:** changing the sold listing back restores only the listings this sale changed, if nobody changed them since, and puts the item's status back. A change made in the same save wins.
  - **Quantity above 1:** no cascade, because the other listings are still needed. The confirmation explains this.
  - Marking two sales at once, or a second sale while one exists (quantity 1), is refused with a clear message.
- **Bug found and fixed while testing:** un-marking a sale restored the other listings, but the same save then wrote their old submitted values back over the restore. The grid now writes only the rows the user changed.

## Summary

Cody can now log business trips and expenses from his phone. My office has a **Log a trip or an expense** button. Trips take a date, where to and the miles, and the regular post-office run is one tap with **Same as last time**. Expenses take what, how much, a category and an optional receipt photo. Both go straight into the year-end tax export as **Mileage** and **Expenses** files. Phase 5 is under way, following the plan Liam approved (decision 197).

## What was done

- **Log a trip:** date (today), trip (suggests trips used before), miles, optional note. The **Same as last time** button logs the previous trip again, dated today.
- **Log an expense:** date, what for, amount, category, optional receipt photo or PDF, optional note. The categories are Supplies, Packing materials, Fees and subscriptions, and Other.
- **Receipt photos** are prepared like item photos: turned into JPEG, upright, location (GPS) data removed. They're kept private, so only signed-in accounts can open them.
- **Latest trips and expenses** are listed underneath, each with Edit and Delete (and Receipt when there is one).
- **Year-end tax export** in My office: each year now has four files, Sales, Donations, Mileage and Expenses. The Mileage file has a total; the Expenses file has a total per category and overall.
- Miles are recorded as driven: the site doesn't work out any deduction.
- **New module:** iShop daily life (ishop_life). It will hold the rest of Phase 5. It gave logged-out visitors no permissions.

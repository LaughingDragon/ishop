## Summary

Depop has an official bulk upload (Selling Hub, then Drafts), and the site now feeds it. **/post/depop-bulk** makes one CSV of every item still "To post" on Depop that Depop allows, plus a ZIP of their photos (one folder per row, already Depop-sized). Each row becomes a Depop draft. The Vinted half of this roadmap line needs Liam's decision: Vinted has no bulk upload (item 154).

## What was done

- **/post/depop-bulk** (also linked from the Depop tab of **Post this item**): lists what's in the CSV and what's left out, with the reason, so it can be fixed on the item. Download buttons for the CSV and the photos, plus the four upload steps.
- The listing text is the same Depop text as on the Post page: title, description, details and flaws, hashtags, within 1000 characters. Conditions use Depop's own words (Brand new / Like new / Used - Good / Used - Fair), ported from the old app's CSV script, as are the columns.
- **Columns:** the old app's list from August, which Depop has changed before. Cody is asked to paste the column row from Depop's real template (item 153), and one constant changes if they differ.
- **Photos ZIP:** folders `01-item-…`, `02-item-…` match the CSV's photo_folder column, each with up to 4 photos at Depop's 1280×1280 size.
- After posting, Cody pastes each listing link into the item's Listings and sets Depop to Posted. The item then leaves the list.
- **Vinted:** checked 2026-10-15. There is no bulk upload for normal Vinted sellers, only paid third-party tools. Vinted is already covered by the Post this item page. Recommendation to Liam: drop the Vinted CSV (item 154).

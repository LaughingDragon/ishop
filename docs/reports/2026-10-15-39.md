## Summary

My office now has a **Money** section: how much sold, on which platform, the profit after fees, shipping and what the item cost, how long things take to sell, and which live listings have sat longest. It covers this season, this month, this year, last year or all time. It uses real data only. The front desk's "This season" scoreboard and the My office glance now use the same calculation, so the numbers always agree.

## What was done

- **Totals:** Sold, Sales, Profit, Average days to sell, in the scoreboard's gold digits. Two by two on the phone, four across on a computer.
- **Breakdown line:** for example "This season: $80.00 in sales, minus $13.00 fees, $5.00 shipping and $30.00 item cost."
- **Profit** = sale price − platform fees − shipping paid − what the item cost (the plan Liam approved).
- **Missing data is flagged, never guessed.** A sale with no sale price adds nothing and is flagged; so is one whose item has no cost (its profit may be too high).
- **Sold by platform:** each platform's count, sales and profit, written out in words, with a gold bar for size. The bar never stands alone.
- **Longest-sitting live listings:** the top 5, oldest posting first, each linking to its item.
- **Average days to sell** runs from "Date posted" to the new "Date sold" (step 2).
- **Period buttons** at the top. The choice is in the link, so a period can be bookmarked.

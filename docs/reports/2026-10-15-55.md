## What was done

- **Reading the file:**
  - Takes the zip as Amazon sends it (including a zip inside the zip) or the order-history CSV inside it. Columns are found by name, so a reordered file still reads. A file that isn't an order history is refused with a plain message.
  - Digital orders (their own files, and digital order numbers) and cancelled lines are left out and counted.
  - One product split across two shipments of the same order becomes one purchase with the combined quantity.
- **Privacy (decision 241):**
  - The uploaded file is deleted as soon as it's read, and nothing is saved as a file on the site.
  - Only name, date, price, tax, quantity, order number and ASIN are kept, while the page is open. Addresses, payment details, tracking and gift messages are never kept.
  - Once the items are made, the list is dropped.
- **Picking:**
  - One big tickable card per purchase, newest first, showing the date, the price each with tax, the quantity and "Return by about …".
  - Purchases still inside their return window are ticked. Older ones sit behind **Show older purchases too** (up to 1,000 shown).
  - Anything already in the site (same order number and ASIN) is left out, and the page says how many. Re-importing a newer file never duplicates.
- **Each item made:**
  - title from the product name, cut at a word to 80 characters;
  - draft status;
  - cost paid = price + tax for one;
  - original price;
  - purchase date, order ID, ASIN and quantity;
  - Amazon as the source;
  - return-by about 30 days (holiday rule applied), filled in by step 1.
- **Theme fix found along the way:** the outlined "secondary" button style never applied to form buttons (input.form-submit won), so "Start over" and the AI helper's "Dismiss" showed as gold primary buttons. Both are now outlined. This is a CSS specificity fix only; Cody's design is unchanged.

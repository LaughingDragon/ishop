## What was done

- **Default return-by:**
  - When an item has a purchase date but no return-by, the return-by fills in when the item is saved. It's about 30 days from the order date.
  - Orders from Nov 1 to Dec 31 get until Jan 31 (the holiday rule).
  - If the purchase date changes, a filled-in date follows it. A date Cody typed himself is never changed.
- **On screen:**
  - The item page shows "Return by: about 2026-11-14 (counted from the order date)" for a filled-in date, and just the date for a typed one.
  - The edit form says the date counts from the order date, that Amazon counts from delivery (usually later), so the reminder comes a little early, and links to the settings.
- **Settings** (My office > **Return windows**): days to return (30), and the holiday rule on or off with its three dates. A badly typed date is refused.
- **Reminders:**
  - "Needs you now" lists every item in its last 7 days. **Returned** sets the item to Returned; any live listings become "Take down", as with other finished items. **Keeping it** stops the reminders (a new "Keeping it" tick in the item's purchase section, which can be unticked). **Open** goes to the item.
  - The morning digest names the item only 7 days before, 2 days before and on the last day, not every morning.

## Summary

Bills and subscriptions now remind Cody before they're due. Each one appears in "Needs you now" 3 days ahead (and stays while overdue) with a **Paid** button. Paid moves it to its next due date, or archives a one-off bill. A bill marked as a business expense is also logged in Expenses when paid, so it reaches the year-end tax export.

## What was done

- **My office > Bills and subscriptions:** a "Coming up" list (soonest first) with **Paid**, Edit and Delete, and an **Add a bill or subscription** form: name, amount (optional), next due date, repeats (every month, year or week, or just once), business expense, note.
- **"Needs you now":** for example "Phone, $45.00, due tomorrow" with a gold **Paid** button. It also says "due today", "due yesterday" or "3 days overdue".
- **Paid:**
  - **Every month:** keeps the day of the month. A bill due on the 31st is due on Feb 28 (29 in leap years), then the 31st again.
  - **Every year / every week:** move on a year or a week.
  - **Just once:** archived.
  - **Every time:** the history records when it was paid.
- **Business bills** are logged in Expenses as "Fees and subscriptions" when paid.

## What was done

- **Webform 6.3.1** (stable, Drupal 11 compatible; checked with a dry run first) plus Webform UI, so Build > Source works for Liam.
- **Default "contact" form fixed:** Webform's built-in contact form accepted **anonymous** submissions for about 2 minutes after install (it uses its own access rules, not permissions). Closed, archived, and create access removed; now 403. The access log shows only this VM's own test requests.
- **Form `ask_cody`** ("Help design your site"):
  - **Elements:** pasted from `ask-cody-webform.yml` as given, plus one `actions` element for the "Send my answers" button. Webform 6.3 has no submit-label setting.
  - **Settings, from the YAML's comment block:**
    - wizard progress bar with percentage, no page titles;
    - Back / Next buttons;
    - drafts for logged-in users, auto-saved on every Next, with "welcome back" and "saved" messages;
    - nothing required;
    - a confirmation page with the given thank-you text and a link back to the site office.
  - **Access:** create = theowner and webmaster; view and update own = theowner; view all = webmaster. site-foreman and anonymous visitors: 403.
  - **Phone styling** (in the form's own CSS):
    - every answer is a full-width 52 px card with the box inside, and a ticked answer turns safety yellow;
    - big text boxes and 52 px buttons;
    - the 24-step progress bar is shown as small dots, because numbered circles ran off a phone screen.
  - **No admin tabs** (Test, Results, Build...) above the form. Cody's administrator role would otherwise show them.
  - The progress-bar stylesheet Webform loads **from a CDN** is now hosted locally (`web/libraries/progress-tracker`, MIT). There are no outside requests, and it's ready for an enforced CSP.
- **Status board:** a callout with a big yellow button for whoever may answer:
  - "Help design your site (about 10 min)" at first;
  - "Continue: help design your site" once there's a draft;
  - "Thanks for your style answers" once sent.
- **Results page** `/site-office/ask-cody/results`, for the webmaster only:
  - each submission shows as questions and answers, newest first, marked "draft, in progress" or "sent", with the time in Pacific;
  - Cody, as an administrator, would pass Webform's own "view all" check, so the page checks the form's named view-all list instead.
- **Punch item 97** for Cody, "Answer the style questions", with phone steps.
- **Timezones:** the webmaster and site-foreman accounts were on UTC, so times showed 7 hours ahead. Both are now America/Los_Angeles, like the site and Cody. Liam can change his under My account.

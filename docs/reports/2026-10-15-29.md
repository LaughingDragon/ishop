## Summary

Each item now has a **Post this item** page. It has one tab per platform, every field ready to paste and already cut to that platform's limits, a **Copy** button on each field, and the photos resized the way that platform wants them. On the phone, one tap saves those photos into the Photos app; there's also a ZIP download for the computer. **Previous / Next** step through the items still waiting to go up on that platform. Phase 3 is now 3 of 6.

## What was done

- **Post this item** (gold button above an item's Listings, or `/item/N/post`):
  - Opens on the first platform the item still needs to go up on (one it's allowed on).
  - Tabs show each platform's listing status. A crossed-out name means the rules say "Not allowed", and that tab explains why.
  - Fields come from the old crosslister's formatters, ported word for word: limits, condition names per platform, fee notes and tips. To prove the port, 1,500 random items were run through the old Python code and the new site code: all 9,000 platform packs came out identical. A sample is kept as an automatic test.
  - Character counts on every limited field. If something can't fit, it's flagged in red. Flaws are never the part that gets cut: the free-text description is shortened first.
  - If the item still needs a real title, description, price and so on, the page says so at the top.
  - Photos: Poshmark 1200, Mercari 1080 and Depop 1280 px squares, padded with white (never cropped). Grailed and Vinted get 1600 px and Facebook 1440 px on the long edge. Only as many photos as the platform allows; the page says if any were left out.
  - **Save photos to phone** uses the iPhone share sheet ("Save N Images"). **Download all (ZIP)** gives 01.jpg, 02.jpg, ... in posting order.
- **Eligibility chips** (from the previous session, not reported until now): a green/amber/grey chip per platform on each listing, with tap-for-reason. The rules and reasons are ported word for word.
- New module: **Image Effects 4.0** (needed for the white square padding). It gave anonymous visitors no permissions.
- Housekeeping: the mail settings no longer drift from what's saved in git. Webform removed one of its own settings after Cody's Gmail setup; sending is unchanged. Coding-standards clean-up of the eligibility rules (layout only).

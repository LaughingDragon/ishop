## Summary

Cody sells household things that are already in the house, so everything built on the idea of buying to resell is gone: the Amazon import, return windows and the purchase fields. Phase 6 shows as **Removed** on the board. The Money page and tax export now lead with **Earned after fees**. "What I paid (if you remember)" is optional and never flagged. The AI listing helper switched to the cheaper setup, and a new **Cleared to sell** checkbox now holds back posting. The off-server backup plan is retired. The three real items (121, 255, 256) lost nothing they had filled in.

## What was done

**Removed**
- Import from Amazon: the /add/amazon page and its link on Add item, the Amazon order-file parser, the form, its CSS, routes and tests.
- Return windows: the return-by date and its 30-day default, the holiday-returns setting (My office > Return windows) and its config, the **Returned** and **Keeping it** buttons, and the return warnings in Needs you now and the morning digest.
- Item fields: order ID, ASIN, purchase source (with its vocabulary), purchase date, return-by and "keep" flag. Their storage was purged cleanly (update 11004). Also removed: the status **Returned to Amazon**, here and in Stockroom bulk actions.
- Punch items 242 and 251 closed: "Not needed: the Amazon import was removed." The note tells Cody to delete Amazon's file when it arrives.
- Backups: the off-server bucket scripts and units (ishop-offsite, ishop-restore-check), the "10 real items" reminder and the board's off-server check. Decision 268: Google Cloud daily snapshots of both disks (7-day retention) plus the nightly 02:15 site backups are the plan.

**Changed**
- "Cost paid" is now **What I paid (if you remember)**: optional, and not in any checklist or reminder.
- Money page and tax export: **Earned after fees** (sale price minus fees and shipping) is the main number. Profit shows only where "What I paid" was entered. A missing sale price is still flagged; a blank "What I paid" never is.
- Original price (retail value) is kept.
- AI prompt: the items are pre-owned household goods. Condition says "New with tags" only when tags or sealed packaging are visible.
- AI default (decision 264): Claude Sonnet 5.5, photos at 1092 px, search limited to resale sites (max 4). The price is labelled "An estimate from the photos and current listings, not confirmed sales". A **See eBay sold prices** button sits on the item page and next to the AI price.
- **Cleared to sell** (decision 265): a checkbox in the Listing section of the item form. New items start unticked; 121, 255 and 256 are ticked. Until it's ticked, the item says "Check it's OK to sell first". It is left out of the to-post queue (and so of Depop bulk), its Post this item pages and photo ZIP are held back, Stockroom **Mark posted** skips it, and the listings grid won't set a new **Posted**. Recording a sale, or adding something already live (Add item > already selling), is not blocked.
- Site office: a new phase status, **Removed**. ROADMAP Phase 6 is marked removed with the reason, and Phases 1, 3, 4 and 5 no longer mention the removed parts. Decision 269 records the redesign. TEMPORARY.md had nothing about Phase 6.

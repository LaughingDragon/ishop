## What was done

**Built in Phase 1** (each step has its own report):
- **Data disk:** the extra 10 GB disk `ishopway2muchdata` holds item photos and local backups. The site runs without it and shows "Photo storage offline".
- **Item:** all approved fields; a ready-to-list checklist; title and description counters.
  - Taxonomies: condition, category, brand, storage location (Game room > Table), purchase source.
  - Photos: media in private files. HEIC becomes JPEG, turned upright, metadata stripped, at most 2560 px.
- **Platform listings:** one per item per platform, with their own history.
  - A grid on the item page.
  - "Sold here" asks first, takes down the other live listings, and can be undone exactly.
  - Quantity above 1 means no cascade.
- **Phone flow, `/add`:**
  - **Take photo** / **Choose photos**; photos upload one by one with progress;
  - **Take another / Done** after each camera shot;
  - **Make cover**, up/down and remove on each photo;
  - optional title, price, condition and category;
  - **Save** opens the item.
  - Without JavaScript, a plain multi-photo upload still works.
- **Home-screen app:** a web manifest ("Reseller Matrix", red **RM** icon, opens on `/add`, standalone). No service worker and no offline copy of private data.
- **90-day logins:** both the cookie and the server-side session. Drupal's default server-side limit had been about 2 days of inactivity.

**Today's step 4 finish:**
- **Front-end pages** (Reseller Matrix) no longer show the admin sidebar or grey top bar, for anyone. The site office and admin pages keep them, Cody stays administrator, and crew get an **Admin** button after Account.
- **Questionnaire panel** removed from the status board. The results page stays.
- **Cody uses Chrome on his iPhone** (decision 110). It runs Apple's WebKit underneath, so camera, HEIC and uploads behave as in Safari. His steps use Chrome's Share → Add to Home Screen.
- **`/add`, the listings grid and the checklist** restyled for the dark theme.
- **Found and fixed:**
  - **Thumbnails didn't load:** the private thumbnail folders had been created by a command-line test render under the wrong owner, so the web server couldn't make thumbnails. Fixed, and the private folders now pass their group down.
  - **WebP thumbnails of private photos** were labelled `image/jpeg`: a core quirk, fixed with a small response fix.
  - **Abandoned uploads:** photos uploaded on `/add` but never saved are deleted by cron after 24 hours. Photos used by any item, or in any item's history, are kept.
  - **Brief error page at 12:29:** while the (now parked) crew beacon was being built, the site office expected a service that wasn't there yet. Anyone loading the site office then got an error page. No PHP errors since 12:30.
- **"Add item"** in the header now opens `/add`.

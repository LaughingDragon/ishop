## Summary

The plan for Phase 8: a public **/shop** page listing the items Cody marks "Show in shop", each with photos, description, price and a way to buy. The whole catalog sits behind **one switch that starts off**. While it's off, the site stays exactly as private as it is now: every page asks visitors to sign in. Nothing is built until Liam says OK (item 253). Four questions are below.

## What was done

Nothing built. Where things stand:

- The site is private: a visitor who isn't signed in gets "Access denied" on every page, including the front page.
- Items already have the **Show in shop** tick.
- Photos are private files, with location data stripped on upload.
- Webform is installed. Honeypot, Pathauto, Metatag and Simple XML Sitemap are not.

## What's next

The proposed plan, in three steps.

1. **The catalog and the switch.**
   - The switch is on a "Public shop" page in the site office, off by default.
   - While it's on:
     - **/shop** shows the items in the shop as photo cards with a price, newest first, with a category filter;
     - each item has its own page: photos, title, description, flaws, size, condition and price.
   - The catalog never shows cost paid, order numbers, purchase details, storage location, internal notes, AI notes or anything else from the office.
   - **Photos:** the catalog can't show private files, so it shows public copies of each shop item's photos, sized for the web. The copies exist only while the item is in the shop and the switch is on. They're deleted when either stops.
   - **Off** means /shop and the item pages answer "not found", and no public photos exist.
   - When the shop is on, signed-in Cody still lands on the Reseller Matrix, and visitors to the front page are sent to /shop.
2. **How buyers get in touch** (question 2).
3. **Found by search engines** (question 4):
   - Pathauto gives readable addresses, such as /shop/wooden-puzzle-1000-pieces.
   - Metatag gives each item page a title, description and photo for link previews.
   - Simple XML Sitemap lists the shop pages, and only those.
   - While the switch is off, nothing is listed and robots are told to stay away, as now.

The look uses Cody's Reseller Matrix theme. Tomorrow Cody can say whether he wants the public pages styled differently.

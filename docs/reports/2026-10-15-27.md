## What was done

Links from anywhere else (eBay, typos) are refused with the link shown, and so is more than one link per platform.
  - **Posted on:** six checkboxes, pre-checked with **his last choice** (all six the first time). A pasted link counts as checked.
  - **Title** (required here) and **price** at the top. Condition and category are optional.
  - **Photos are optional.** This mode also works if photo storage is offline.
  - **Saving:** the item becomes **Listed**. Checked and linked platforms are **Posted**, with the link, the price as listed price, and today as date posted. The rest stay "To post".
- **Deviation from the roadmap wording:** "a listing URL paste field per platform" became **one paste box** that recognises the platform. It's quicker on a phone. The roadmap notes this.
- **No scraping:** the site never fetches marketplace pages. It only reads the link's address.

## Summary

Phase 10 part A is built and live: the **Shipping room** (/room/shipping-room) replaces its "Coming soon" page. It lists sold items that haven't gone out yet, oldest sale first. Each one shows its ship-by date, label PDF, weight, package size and packing note. **Shipped** records the tracking number and links to the carrier's tracking page. **Delivered** finishes the sale, and the Money page shows it. A real-use check for Cody is item 303 (with his next real sale). Part B (marketplace inbox) is next.

## What was done

- **Physical Fields 1.5** installed (stable, Drupal 11, covered by security advisories). Items now have **Weight (packed)** (oz or lb) and **Package size** (inches), plus a new **Packing note**, under Shipping on the Edit page. These replace the old weight and package fields, which had no data on any item and were removed (update 11009; database dump taken first).
- **Sold listings carry the shipping details:** ship-by date, label PDF, carrier, tracking number, date shipped and date delivered. Every change is a new listing revision.
- **Ship-by dates:** a new sale gets an *estimate* from the platform's usual handling time (Poshmark 7 days, Mercari 3, eBay 1, Depop 5, Grailed 3, Vinted 5, Facebook 3; setting `ishop_items.shipping`). The room marks it "(estimate: check the app)" until Cody types the real date. Part B (sale emails) and part C (eBay) will fill in the real date automatically. These day counts are my best guesses, not checked against each platform's rules.
- **Label PDF:** uploaded per sale, stored as a private file. Only Cody and Liam can open it; a visitor gets "Access denied". Because it has the buyer's address, it is **deleted 30 days after delivery** (or 60 days after shipping if never marked Delivered), on cron. Replacing a label deletes the old one.
- **Shipped:** type or paste the tracking number. The carrier is worked out from the number (USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL), or Cody picks it when the site can't tell. A scanned USPS barcode's "420 + ZIP" prefix is dropped, so the buyer's ZIP code is never stored. Postage paid goes into "Shipping paid" (Money). If there's no tracking number (handed over in person), Cody ticks a box to say so.
- **On the way:** "Track on USPS/UPS/FedEx/DHL" opens the carrier's own public page (no carrier API, no account). **Delivered** and **Undo** are protected links.
- **Front desk:** "Needs you now" shows up to 3 sales to ship, most urgent first ("Ship X (sold on Mercari): Ship by tomorrow."), plus "N more". The room tile shows "N sales to ship, ship by …". The morning digest picks these up too, because it reuses the same rows.
- **Money page:** totals still count sales by date sold, so the tax export and season numbers don't change when a parcel arrives. A new line shows how many sales are delivered, on the way and still to ship, with earnings for each.
- Phase 10 added to ROADMAP and the board (A ticked). Decisions 300 (Phase 10 approved), 302 (label privacy and Money). Straggler from item 295: Phase 8 marked Done (Liam signed off 292; decision 301).

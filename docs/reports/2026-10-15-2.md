## What was done

- Security Kit:

  - Referrer-Policy strict-origin-when-cross-origin.
  - X-Frame-Options SAMEORIGIN.
  - Content security policy in report-only mode (violations go to the log).
- Login flood limits: 5 failed logins per account per 6 hours, 20 per IP per hour. Flood Control module for unblocking.
- Two-factor (TFA 1.13): installed and configured with TOTP, recovery codes and a 30-day trusted browser, but **not required**, by decision.

- Private by default: anonymous users have no permissions, the 403 page is the login form, and only admins can create accounts.

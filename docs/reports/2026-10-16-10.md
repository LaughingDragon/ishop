## What was done

- **/shop**: photo cards with price and condition, newest first, and category buttons once there are two or more categories.
- **Item pages** at readable addresses, such as /shop/wooden-puzzle-1000-pieces-121. Each shows photos, title, description, flaws, size, condition and price. An address with the wrong words redirects to the right one.
- **Buying**: links to Cody's live listings only (status Posted, with that platform's link), for example "Buy on Poshmark". There is no contact form, and his email address never shows. An item with no live listing says "Not on a marketplace yet."
- **Which items show:** "Show in shop" is ticked, the item is Ready or Listed, "Cleared to sell" is ticked, and it has a price and at least one photo.
- **Never shown:** cost paid, storage location, internal notes, AI notes, price checks, or anything from the office. The public pages read only a fixed list of fields.
- **Photos:** while the shop is on, each shop item gets public copies of its photos: 1600 px, metadata stripped again, and names that don't reveal anything. A copy is deleted once its item leaves the shop or the switch goes off. In a preview, the private photos are shown instead (only signed-in users can open them).
- **Search engines and link previews:** each page has a description, Open Graph tags (title, description, photo) and a canonical address. /shop/sitemap.xml lists the shop pages and only those, and only while the shop is on. These are built in rather than added as Pathauto, Metatag and Simple XML Sitemap, because those modules work on every page, the private ones too (decision 291).
- **The switch:** Site office > **Public shop** (/site-office/public-shop), for Cody and Liam only, with a confirm tick. Turning it on or off makes or deletes the public photos at once.
- **While on:** visitors to the front page go to /shop. Signed-in Cody still lands on the Reseller Matrix.
- The look is Cody's Reseller Matrix theme.

## Summary

Anything that has been up on a platform for 30+ days without selling now gets a nudge: about 10% off at 30 days, shown as the new price, or "consider donating" after 90. The three most overdue appear in "Needs you now", each with **Done**, **Snooze 2 weeks** and **Keep as is**, so the panel never floods. The rest are one tap away.

## What was done

- **The rule** (Liam's spec): days are counted per platform from that listing's "Date posted". At 30+ days: "Try $27 (about 10% off)". The price comes from the item's asking price, rounded down to whole dollars, so cuts never compound. At 90+ days: "Consider donating it".
- **"Needs you now"**: up to 3 rows, most overdue first, then "N more listings have been up 30+ days. See them all".
- **Done:** records the new price on that listing. On a "consider donating" nudge, Done marks the item **Donated**, and its live listings come up as "take it down from …" rows.
- **Snooze 2 weeks:** hides that nudge for 14 days.
- **Keep as is:** hides it until the next step (the 90-day donate nudge), or for good after that.
- **/post/stale:** everything that needs a nudge, with the same buttons, plus listings whose price is already at or below the suggestion.
- On the item page, each stale listing shows a line such as "Up 45 days: try about 10% off ($27)".
- **Daily cron** clears out answers for listings that are no longer live. Answers are kept per listing as data, not config.
- Donated, kept and returned items now also drop out of the to-post queues and the Depop CSV.
- Every listing that goes live now gets its "Date posted" automatically, so the 30-day clock always starts.

  - Pass: commit and file a report. Fail: full rollback, a punch list item and a report.
  - Drupal core is limited to patch releases (`~11.4.0`).
  - Dumps go to /var/backups/ishop-update (last 4 kept) and logs to /var/log/ishop-update (last 26).
- **Disk move** (`scripts/move-to-data-disk.sh`):
  - Formats only a blank disk.
  - Moves the site and database onto it, bind-mounted back at the same paths.
  - Keeps backups on the system disk, and the old copies as `*.pre-move`.
  - Services require the mounts. `--dry-run` prints the plan.

## What was done

**1. Certain sales mark themselves.**
- A match is certain when the email carries the listing's own link, or its title matches exactly one item on the site (score 0.9 or more, every other item under 0.6) and that item has a listing on the sending platform.
- The listing must be Posted (or Posted - at risk), and the price within 10% of the listed price when both are known.
- The site then marks it Sold here with the take-down cascade and records the price, the email's date, the label and the real ship-by.
- The front desk shows "Sold on Vinted: <item>, $18.00. Take it down on Mercari, Poshmark." with **Undo** (7 days) and **OK**.
- Undo puts the listing and the other sites back, and removes the price and any label the inbox added.
- A label email that arrives after a sale question was asked joins that question; it never marks the sale by itself.

**2. The same rule for other emails:**
- **Shipped:** Vinted "on its way" and Mercari "item is on its way", with the tracking number when the email has one (USPS or UPS).
- **Delivered:** Mercari "package has arrived" or "order completed", Vinted "This order is completed". Also marks it shipped if it wasn't. Vinted's "Transferred to your Vinted Wallet: $X" is recorded as fees = sold price minus payout.
- **Payouts with an amount** on a sale with no fees yet: fees recorded the same way. Nothing is guessed when there's no sold price or the payout is higher than it.
- **Returns, cases, disputes:** never automatic. They show "Answer by <date>" when the email gives a reply-by date.
- **"Your listing is live":** fills the listing link only when the email contains the listing's own link and the row has none. No platform sent one in the last 60 days (only Poshmark "Just Listed" promotions, which don't count).
- Anything less certain asks with one tap: Mark shipped, Mark delivered, Record payout, Save link.

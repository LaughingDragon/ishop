## Summary

**Write my listing** now runs in two steps (Liam approved it in chat). A cheap model writes the whole draft from one photo. The price search, which is most of the cost, only runs for items it values at $15 or more. In real tests on Cody's three items, an $8 item cost **$0.0005** and the two over $15 cost **$0.056** each, against $0.30-$0.64 on the original setup.

## What was done

- **Step 1:** Claude Haiku 5.5 looks at the first photo at 1092 px and fills in title, description, category (site list only), condition, flaws, brand, color and a rough price. No search. About 2,800 tokens in, 350 out.
- **Step 2, only from $15 up:** a Claude Sonnet 5.5 sold-price search for the identified item. It is text only, so no photos are sent again. About 21,000 tokens in, with one search.
- **Reuse:** when a near-identical item (same title and category, ignoring case and punctuation) was researched in the last 30 days, its prices and sources are reused and nothing new is paid for. Why this price says so.
- **Under $15:** Why this price says "A quick estimate from the photo. Items under $15 get no price search ... Check sold prices below for real sales."
- **Safety net:** if step 1 fails, the site runs the previous one-step version automatically, and both costs count.
- Settings (config `ishop_ai.settings`): `two_step: true`, `research_threshold: 15`, `research_reuse_days: 30`. The monthly $32 cap and the cost on the Money page are unchanged.

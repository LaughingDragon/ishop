## Summary

The Stockroom can now be searched and filtered: title, brand, size, status, which platform it's posted on, location and price. On the phone the filters fold into one "Search and filters" panel at the top. A filtered list has its own link, and the bulk actions work on just what's shown. As Liam decided, there are no QR labels or tagging (decision 176), and the filters are built in rather than using Search API (decision 178).

## What was done

- **Search and filters** at the top of /room/stockroom:
  - **Search titles.**
  - **Brand, Size, Status:** drop-downs that list only what the items actually use.
  - **Posted on:** items live on that platform.
  - **Location:** a place includes everything inside it, so "Game room" covers its tables.
  - **Min $ and Max $.**
  - Big fields and buttons: **Show items** and **Clear filters**.
- The panel opens by itself while filters are on, and shows how many. The list says "Showing 2 of 4 items (filtered)". **Select all** picks only the items shown.
- Filters stay in place through **Review changes**, **Back** and **Apply**. The link only names the filters in use (for example `?size=M`).
- **Storage location** stays a plain field on each item, shown as a path such as "Game room > Table".
- **Fix:** item changes saved by the site (bulk actions, finishing an item, the sale cascade) now record the right time in their history. Cody's first bulk move had shown the old time.
- **Cody's check (item 173):** "Done and keep up the fantastic work". His puzzle was moved with a real bulk action on his iPhone, and the change saved.

## Summary

Cody can now drop a pile of photos at once and get one draft item per item photographed. The site groups the photos by when they were taken (a new item after a gap of 45 minutes or more, adjustable) and shows the groups for him to check, split or merge. "Also write each listing with AI" queues an AI draft for every new item. It's linked from "Add item" as "Many items at once? Use the bulk photo drop" (/add/bulk).

## What was done

- **When each photo was taken:** the camera's time is read from the photo when it uploads, before the location and other hidden data are stripped. It's kept on the photo. When a photo has no camera time (some screenshots and edited copies), the phone's file time is used.
- **The page (/add/bulk):**
  - **Choose photos** takes any number. They upload one at a time, which is easier on the phone's connection.
  - Groups appear as **Item 1, Item 2…**, each with the photos and the time the first one was taken.
  - **Start a new item here** on any photo and **Merge with the next item** on any group.
  - A **New item after a gap of 45 minutes** box regroups the photos instantly.
  - The **Create N items** button stays at the bottom of the screen and waits until every upload is done.
- **Creating:** one draft item per group, with its photos in order. Each is titled "New item" plus when the first photo was taken (for example "New item 2026-10-14 10:00"), so they're easy to tell apart in the Stockroom. Then the page goes to the Stockroom, showing drafts.
- **AI:**
  - "Also write each listing with AI" is ticked by default and shows the cost (about $0.40 an item, within the $25 monthly cap).
  - It queues one run per item. The runs go one after another every minute.
  - If AI is paused or at the cap, they wait, as before.
- **Safety:** the server re-checks every photo: it must be his own upload, not already on an item, and not used twice. If the photo drive is offline, the page says so instead of failing.

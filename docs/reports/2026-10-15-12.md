# 2026-10-15: Phase 1 proposal: the item model and phone app (for approval, nothing built)

- Date: 2026-10-15
- Phase: 1 (Phase 1: the item model and phone app)
- Site office node: 77

## Summary

This is the proposal for Phase 1, and nothing is built yet. Each **Item** is a content type with photos as Media. **Platform listings** are a small custom entity (one row per item per platform), not Paragraphs. There are four taxonomies plus a fifth for purchase source, plus a phone "Add item" page that can be saved to the home screen.

Photos are converted from HEIC to JPEG, turned the right way up, stripped of location data and resized to 2560 px on upload. Three things should be settled before building: the boot disk needs to grow, the PWA approach (the PWA module is beta-only on Drupal 11), and the decisions listed below.

## What's next

**Proposal: build only after approval.**

### 1. Item (content type)
**Required fields:**
- **On the phone "Add item" page:** only **one or more photos**. The title defaults to "New item 2026-10-15 14:05", so Cody can snap and go.
- **Before an item counts as "ready to list":** title, description, category, condition, asking price and one or more photos. The item page shows what's missing as a checklist; the form doesn't block saving.

**The fields** (the roadmap's list, plus the suggested additions):
- **Title.** A counter warns past 60 (Grailed) and 70 (Vinted) characters, with a hard limit of 80.
- **Photos.** Media (image), as many as needed, drag to reorder; the first photo is the cover.
- **Description.** Plain text, with a counter at 1000.
- **Flaws / condition notes.** Plain text.
- **Brand.** Taxonomy; new brands are added by typing them.
- **Size.** Free text. The old data is too varied for a fixed list.
- **Color** *(suggested addition)*. Free text; it was on nearly every old item.
- **Category.** Taxonomy, two levels.
- **Condition.** Taxonomy with six fixed terms.
- **Prices:**
  - asking price;
  - AI price range (min and max);
  - original retail price *(suggested addition: Poshmark asks for it)*.
- **Purchase details:**
  - cost paid;
  - purchase date;
  - purchase source (taxonomy, see section 3);
  - order ID;
  - return-by date.
- **Storage location.** Taxonomy.
- **Internal notes.** Plain text.
- **Show in shop.** A yes/no flag, off by default (used in Phase 8).
- **Crosslister ID** *(suggest dropping)*. Phase 2 is a fresh start with no import, so it's never used.
- **Layout:** fields are grouped with Field Group into "Listing", "Money and purchase" and "Storage and notes", so the phone form stays short.

### 2. Platform listings: custom entity, not Paragraphs
Each listing holds:
- the item and platform (Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Grailed, Vinted, Facebook);
- the status (To post / Posted / Sold here / Take down / Not needed - sold / Posted - at risk / Not allowed);
- the listing URL, listed price and date posted;
- the sold price and fees;
- the eligibility override (yes / no / check) and a note.

Each item can have only one listing per platform.

**Why a custom entity and not Paragraphs:**
- **Listings are searched on their own, across items.** "Everything still To post on Depop", "Take down list", "Sold this month with fees" are the views Phases 3–4 depend on. Paragraphs can be searched, but they're built as parts of one page, and those queries get clumsy.
- **Statuses change often and separately.** With Paragraphs, every status change saves a new revision of the whole item. With an entity, each listing keeps its own small history.
- **One per item per platform** is easy to enforce on an entity. Paragraphs can't enforce it.
- **Phone editing:** the item page gets a compact listings grid (one row per platform, status dropdown, URL, price). That's lighter than nested Paragraphs forms.
- **The cost:** about a day more code than Paragraphs, all in a custom module with tests.

**Suggested behaviour:**
- Creating an item creates its six listing rows as "To post".
- Marking one listing "Sold here" moves the item's other "Posted" rows to "Take down", and its "To post" rows to "Not needed - sold".
- The old app's eligibility rules (which platforms allow what) belong to Phase 3, "selling helpers".

### 3. Taxonomies and starting terms
- **Condition:** New with tags, New without tags, Like new, Good, Fair, Poor. Mapping to each platform's own words is Phase 3.
- **Category, top level:**

### 4. Photos
- **Media type "Item photo".** Stored in **private** files (`private://items/YYYY-MM/`), since the whole site is private; served only to logged-in crew.
- **On upload:**
  - detect HEIC/HEIF by the file's contents, not its name, and convert it to JPEG (quality 88);
  - turn photos the right way up;
  - **strip location data** (GPS) and other metadata;
  - shrink so the long edge is at most 2560 px (never enlarged).
  - Tools: the Debian `imagemagick` package (it uses the HEIC library that's already installed), through the ImageMagick toolkit module 5.0.1.
- **Reorder:** drag in the item form. On a phone, the "Show row weights" fallback gives number boxes.
- **Platform-sized copies** come later (Phase 3), as image styles: square padded for Poshmark, Mercari and Depop; 1600 px for Grailed and Vinted.
- **Disk:**
  - Each photo is about 0.5–1.5 MB, so 3 per item is about 3 MB.
  - **500 items is about 1.5 GB, and 2.4 GB is free.** Proposal: grow the boot disk from 10 to 30 GB. Liam does that in the console; Claude grows the partition.
  - Disks can grow but never shrink, so 30, not 100.
- **Backups:**
  - The nightly private-files tarball (14 kept) would copy every photo 14 times.
  - Proposal: leave item photos out of that tarball, and copy them daily, as additions only, to the backup bucket from punch item 73.

### 5. Phone "Add item" flow and the home-screen app
- **`/add`**, also the home-screen icon's start page:
  - two big buttons, **Take photos** (opens the camera) and **Choose photos** (the library);
  - photos upload one by one with progress and thumbnails, and can be dragged to reorder;
  - optional quick fields: title, asking price, condition, category;
  - **Save** opens the item with its "what's missing" checklist.
  - The site office header gets an **Add item** button.
- **Home-screen app:**
  - **Recommend a small custom module rather than PWA 2.x beta.** It provides a web manifest (name, start page `/add`, standalone display, safety-yellow theme colour, hard-hat icons at 192 and 512 px, Apple touch icon).
  - On iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android Chrome offers "Install".
  - **No offline caching.** It would keep private data on the phone, and stale pages could confuse things. If Android insists on a service worker before offering Install, add a minimal one that caches nothing.

### 6. Permissions

- **Cost, order ID and internal notes** are visible to the three office accounts only. The site is private anyway.
- **site-foreman** gets the new item and listing permissions, but not user management.

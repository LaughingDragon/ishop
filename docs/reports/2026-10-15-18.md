# 2026-10-15: Design brief from Cody's answers

- Date: 2026-10-15
- Phase: 1 (Phase 1: the item model and phone app)
- Site office node: 101

## Summary

Cody is the only designer (decision 100), and his questionnaire answers are the brief. In short: a **dark, playful, well-run shop** called the **Reseller Matrix**. It's an office building with all seven rooms, opening on a dashboard that shows every room at a glance and greets him with "Welcome back, boss".

The colours are **forest green and tan, burgundy and gold, red and gold**, and never blue with green. Text is a bit bigger, buttons have an icon with a word under it, and the logo is just the name in nice lettering. The site's personality is **punctual, attentive and polite**. He uses his iPhone and a computer about equally.

## What was done

Read Cody's sent answers (submission 2, 13:13), and turned them into this brief:
- **Front page:** named **"Reseller Matrix"**. A custom dashboard showing all the rooms at once, greeting him with **"Welcome back, boss"**.
- **Rooms:** he'd use all seven: Front desk, Stockroom, Shipping room, My office, Mailroom, Calendar wall and Break room. He loves the office-building idea.
- **Feel:** like a well-run shop, and playful ("have fun with it").
- **Light or dark:** dark.
- **Colours he likes:** forest green and tan; burgundy and gold; red and gold (his own addition).
- **Colours he dislikes:** blue and green together.
- **Text size:** a bit bigger.
- **Buttons:** a small picture with a word under it.
- **Logo:** the name in nice lettering; no mascot.
- **Personality:** "punctual, attentive, polite", which fits the front-desk secretary persona idea.
- **His ideal office:** "an open field with a few trees and a creek running through the side. Pretty like Switzerland." This is a good source for imagery: meadow greens, warm tan, gold light.
- **Device:** iPhone and computer about equally, so both layouts matter.
- **Reminders:** a notice on his phone when something needs him.
- **Other users:** maybe a family member helping out someday.
- **Pet peeve:** being asked to log in more than once.
- **Looks he admires:** none named.

## Decisions needed

1. **Staying logged in (his pet peeve).** On an iPhone, a home-screen app keeps its own login, separate from Safari's, so he'd log in once more there. After that it should stay logged in for a long time. Proposal: sessions last 90 days on his devices, and the home-screen app opens straight to the dashboard.
2. **Phone notices instead of (or as well as) the morning email.** iPhones show web notifications only for a site added to the home screen (iOS 16.4 or later), and that needs a small service worker for notifications only, with no offline copy of private data. That changes the Phase 1 manifest plan (no service worker) and the Phase 5 digest (email). Proposal: build the manifest so notifications can be added in Phase 5, with email as the fallback.
3. **A family member helping out:** a limited "helper" role, later, when it happens.
4. **Order of work:** make the looks now, or finish Phase 1 step 4 (the phone "Add item" page) first?

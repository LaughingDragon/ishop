## What's next

The proposed plan, in three steps.

1. **Return-by by default.**
   - Whenever an item gets a purchase date (on "Add item", the edit form or the import), return-by fills in as purchase date + 30 days unless Cody sets it himself.
   - On the warning row: **Returned** sets the item to Returned, and **Keeping it** stops the warnings for that item.
2. **Amazon import** (Add item > **Import from Amazon**):
   - Cody uploads the "Your Orders" file from Amazon's **Request your data**: the zip as emailed, or the order-history CSV inside it.
   - A preview lists each purchase: name, date, price, quantity, and whether it's still returnable.
   - Purchases still inside their return window are ticked; older ones aren't. Cancelled orders are left out.
   - Anything already imported (same order and product) is shown as "already in" and can't be added twice. Re-importing a newer file is safe.
   - Each ticked line becomes a draft item:
     - title from the product name (cut to 80 characters);
     - cost paid = the price plus tax for one unit;
     - original price, purchase date, order ID, ASIN and quantity, with Amazon as the source;
     - return-by = the purchase date + 30.
   - No photos come over (Amazon's export has none). Cody adds them as usual, and "Write my listing" works once there are photos.
3. **Warnings at 7 and 2 days:**
   - The digest names an item when its window is 7 days and 2 days from closing, and on the last day. It doesn't repeat every morning.
   - The "Needs you now" row stays on the front desk for the whole last week, with the two buttons.

Cody's part: request his Amazon data once it's approved. Amazon usually emails it within a few days. I'll add a punch item for him with the steps. Liam isn't asked to do anything but decide.

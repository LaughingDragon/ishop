## Details

Phase 6 step 2: /add/amazon. Built and tested with a made-up file in Amazon's layout; Cody's real file is the final check before sign-off.

## Instructions

**Who:** Cody, on your iPhone in **Safari**. **When:** after Amazon's "your data is ready" email has come and you've downloaded **Your Orders** (item 242). **Time:** about 5 minutes.

1. Open the site and tap **Add item**. Under **Photos**, tap **Import from Amazon**.
2. Tap **Choose File**, then **Browse** and **Downloads**, and pick the **Your Orders** zip. Tap **Read the file**.
3. The page lists the purchases that aren't in the site yet, newest first:
   - the ones still inside their return window are ticked;
   - **Show older purchases too** adds the rest;
   - cancelled and digital orders are left out.
4. Untick anything you're keeping for yourself. Tick older things you want to resell.
5. Tap **Add the ticked ones as drafts**. They appear in the Stockroom as drafts, with what you paid, the order date and a return-by date of about 30 days. Add photos and a price when you have each one in hand.
6. The uploaded file is deleted straight away. You can delete it from your phone too. Importing a newer file later never adds the same purchase twice.

From then on, the front desk shows each item in the last week of its return window, with **Returned**, **Keeping it** and **Open** buttons.

**How to answer:** type **Done** and how many items it added, plus anything that looked wrong (a wrong price, date or name, or something missing). Then tap **Save answer and close**.

## Instructions

**Who:** Cody, on your iPhone in **Safari**. **Time:** 2 minutes. This makes one real change: your puzzle's storage location, which you can set to wherever it really is.

1. Open the site and tap **Stockroom** in the room bar (or the Stockroom tile on the front desk).
2. Under **1. What to do**, tap **Move to a storage location**, then pick where the puzzle really is (for example "Game room > Table > Table A").
3. Under **2. Which items**, tick the puzzle ("New item 2026-10-15 14:29"). The bar at the bottom should say **1 selected**.
4. Tap **Review changes**. You'll see the puzzle's location now, then an arrow, then the new one. Nothing has changed yet.
5. Tap **Apply 1 change**. The page shows **What changed**.

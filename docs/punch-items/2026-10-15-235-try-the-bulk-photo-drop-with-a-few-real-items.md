Phase 7 step 4: /add/bulk splits a pile of photos into items by the time taken (45-minute gap). Tested with marked test photos only.

## Instructions

**Who:** Cody, on your iPhone in **Safari**. **Time:** about 5 minutes, next time you photograph a few items in one go.

The new **bulk photo drop** turns a pile of photos into separate items. It goes by when each photo was taken: whenever there's a gap of 45 minutes or more between photos, it starts a new item.

1. Photograph two or three things you're going to sell, with a break of 45 minutes or more between items. (Or use photos already on your phone from different times.)
2. Open the site and tap **Add item**. Under **Photos**, tap **Many items at once? Use the bulk photo drop**.
3. Tap **Choose photos** and pick all of them at once. They upload one by one, and the page sorts them into **Item 1**, **Item 2** and so on.
4. Check the groups:
   - Two items stuck together? Tap **Start a new item here** on the first photo of the second item.
   - One item split in two? Tap **Merge with the next item**.
   - You can also change the **45 minutes** box.
5. Leave **Also write each listing with AI** ticked if you want the AI to draft each one (about $0.40 an item). Untick it to fill them in yourself.
6. Tap **Create 2 items** (or however many). They appear in the Stockroom as drafts. With AI ticked, each draft listing shows up on its item page within a few minutes.

**How to answer:** type **Done** and how many items it made, plus anything that grouped wrongly or was confusing. Then tap **Save answer and close**.

## Instructions

**Who:** Liam, on an iPhone in **Safari**, signed in as **webmaster**. **Time:** 5 minutes. Cody can't see this item, the settings page or your preview.

1. Turn the iPhone's volume up.
2. Go to **ishoponamazonwaytoomuch.ohbah.com/site-office/birthday**.
3. In **Cody's birthday**, pick his birthday (any year; only the month and day are used). Leave **For everyone** on **Automatic**.
4. Tick **Preview it for me only** and **Play the first-open launch again for me**. Tap **Save**.
5. Tap **Open the dashboard**. A full-screen birthday sign should say **Tap anywhere to start the party**. (iPhone Safari never plays sound before a tap, so this one tap is how Cody's "first open" will start too.) Tap it. The song should start.
6. Let it run past the end (about 2 minutes). It should start over by itself.
7. Tap **Stockroom** in the room bar. The song stops (that's how browsers work when a page changes), and a gold **Resume the party** button floats at the bottom right. Tap it: the song carries on from about where it was.
8. Go back to **/site-office/birthday**, untick **Preview it for me only**, and tap **Save**. The date stays; on the day it switches on by itself, for everyone.

## Instructions

**Who:** Cody. **Time:** about 15 minutes. **Needed by:** Phase 7 (AI listing). Nothing earlier depends on it.

The AI features (writing listings from photos, suggesting prices) will run on **your own** Anthropic account, so you control the cost. It must never be your son's account or key.

1. On a computer, go to **console.anthropic.com** and choose **Sign up**. Use your own email address and verify it from the email they send.
2. Fill in the short profile (your name; for organization, "I Shop on Amazon Way Too Much" is fine).
3. Go to **Settings → Billing**. Add a payment card and buy a small amount of credit, for example $10–$20. The site uses very little: a few cents per item.
4. Go to **Settings → Limits** (sometimes shown as "Spend limits") and set a **monthly spend limit**, for example $20. The account then can't spend more than that in a month, whatever happens.
5. **Don't create an API key yet.** When Phase 7 starts, Liam will tell you how to create one and hand it over safely. A key must never be typed into the site office, an email or a text message.

The screens may be laid out a little differently. If you get stuck, take a screenshot and send it to Liam.

**How to answer:** "Done, limit $___ a month". Don't include card details, keys or passwords. The answer box refuses anything that looks like a key or password.

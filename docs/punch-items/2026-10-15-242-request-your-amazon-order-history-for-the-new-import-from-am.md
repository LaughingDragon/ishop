## Instructions

**Who:** Cody, on your iPhone in **Safari** or on any computer. **Time:** about 3 minutes now. Amazon then emails the file, usually within a few days (they say it can take up to 30).

**Second try.** You said the first request went to a different Amazon account. That's fine: nothing was changed. This time, make sure you are signed in to the Amazon account you actually buy your resale stock with.

1. In **Safari** (not the Amazon app), go to **amazon.com**.
2. Check which account you're in: the top of the page says **Hello, (name)**.
   - If it's the wrong account, tap **Account & Lists** (or the person icon), scroll down and tap **Sign out**, then sign in to the right account.
3. Tap **Account & Lists** (or the person icon), then **Your Account**.
4. Scroll to **Data and privacy** and tap **Request your data**.
   - If you can't find it, search the Amazon help pages for "Request your data".
5. In the list of what to request, choose **Your Orders**, then tap **Submit Request**.
6. Amazon emails you to check it's you. Open that email and tap **Confirm data request**.
   - The email goes to the address on **that** Amazon account, which may be a different inbox from last time.
7. When the "your data is ready" email arrives (in a few days), download the file it links to, **Your Orders** (a .zip).
   - Don't open or unzip it; just keep it in **Files > Downloads**.
   - If a file also arrives from the first (wrong) account, ignore it and don't upload it.

The upload steps are in the next punch item, "Import your Amazon purchases".

**How to answer:** type **Requested** and the name shown after "Hello," (step 2) once you've confirmed the request (step 6). Then tap **Save answer and close**.

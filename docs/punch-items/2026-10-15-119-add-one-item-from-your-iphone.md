## Instructions

**Who:** Cody, on your iPhone in **Safari**. **Time:** 5 minutes.

The icon opens whatever page you added it from. Last time that was this punch item, so it just came back here. Let's add it from the **Add item** page instead.

1. **Remove the old icon:** press and hold the red RM icon, tap **Remove App** (or **Delete Bookmark**), then **Delete from Home Screen**.
2. In Safari, tap the yellow **Add item** button at the top of the page, or go to **ishoponamazonwaytoomuch.ohbah.com/add**. You should see two big buttons, **Take photo** and **Choose photos**.
3. **On that Add item page,** tap **Share** (the square with the arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**. If you see an **Open as Web App** switch, leave it **on**. Tap **Add**.
4. Tap the new RM icon. It should open on **Add item**. Log in if asked; after that it remembers you.
5. Tap **Take photo**. It won't pop up by itself; you tap it. Take the photo and tap **Use Photo**. Tap **Take another** for 1 or 2 more, then **Done**.
6. Tap **Save item**.

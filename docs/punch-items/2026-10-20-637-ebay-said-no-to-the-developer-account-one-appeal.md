## Instructions

eBay turned down your developer account and didn't say why ("problems with the data provided or other irregularities"). It's worth one appeal. If eBay says yes, the site can later read your eBay orders, returns and cases directly. Until then, the site reads eBay's emails to you instead, so you don't lose anything. About 10 minutes.

Other sellers say eBay's first answer to an appeal is often automatic, so don't worry if it comes back as another "no" straight away.

### What to do

1. **Check your details first.** Open the eBay app or ebay.com, go to **My eBay > Account > Personal information**, and note exactly how your **name**, **address** and **phone** are written. The usual reason for a rejection is the developer form not matching your eBay seller account.
2. **Turn off any VPN** on your phone or computer, and use your home internet (not public wifi).
3. Open **https://developer.ebay.com/support/developer-account-support**. Choose **Contact Us**, and pick the option for a **rejected registration** if it asks.
4. Fill in the form with **exactly** the name, address and phone from step 1, and the business Gmail. In the message box, paste this (change anything that isn't right):

   > My eBay Developers Program registration was rejected with "problems with the data provided or other irregularities". I'm an active eBay seller (eBay username: YOUR EBAY USERNAME) and I want API access only for my own seller account: my orders, ship-by dates, returns and cases, for my own small business tool. I don't sell or share data and won't use other sellers' accounts. My details on this form match my eBay account. Please review the registration manually. Thank you.

5. Send it. eBay replies to the business Gmail.
6. If the reply is another automatic "no", or nothing comes within 5 business days, email eBay's developer support team (the address is **developer-support** followed by **@ebay.com**) with the same message and the rejection email's date.

## Instructions

Thanks for trying it. You found a real problem: you typed the **Sold for** price on the Wrangler quarter-zip's Vinted row, but its status stayed **Posted**, so the site didn't know it sold and the Shipping room stayed empty. That's fixed now: typing a Sold for price asks you to mark it sold. Your Wrangler sale still needs one tap to finish, because its price was saved before the fix.

### What to do

1. Open the **Wrangler Authentics Sweater Fleece Quarter-Zip** item (Stockroom, search "Wrangler"). Scroll to **Listings**. On the **Vinted** row, change the status to **Sold here**, tap **Save listings**, then **Yes, mark sold**. (Poshmark will switch to **Take down**: remember to take that listing down in the Poshmark app.)
2. Go to the front page and tap **Shipping room** (the "Ship" tile). Your sale is under **To ship**.
3. Look at the date chip (for example "Ship by Mon Oct 19"). If it says **(estimate: check the app)**, the site guessed it. Tap **Add label PDF**, change **Ship by** to the date the marketplace app shows, and tap **Save**.
4. Get the shipping label from the marketplace app or email and save it as a PDF on your phone. Tap **Add label PDF** (or **Change label or date**), choose the PDF under **Label (PDF)**, and tap **Save**. **Open label (PDF)** then opens it for printing. The site keeps it private and deletes it 30 days after delivery, because it has the buyer's address on it.
5. Optional: if the card says **Not weighed yet** or **No size yet**, tap it and fill in **Weight (packed)**, **Package size** and **Packing note** under **Shipping** on the item's Edit page, then tap **Save**.
6. Pack it and drop it off. Then tap **Shipped**, type or paste the **Tracking number** (leave **Carrier** on "Work it out from the number" unless it asks you), fill in **Postage you paid** if you paid any, and tap **Shipped**.
7. The sale moves to **On the way**. Tap **Track on USPS** (or UPS, FedEx, DHL) to check that the carrier's page opens.
8. When the buyer gets it, tap **Delivered**. It moves to **Delivered**, and **My office > Money** counts it as delivered.

If you tap something by mistake, **Undo Shipped** and **Undo** put it back.

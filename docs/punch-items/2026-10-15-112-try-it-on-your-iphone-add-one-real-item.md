## Instructions

**Who:** Cody, on your iPhone, in **Chrome**. **Time:** about 10 minutes. Have one real item and some packing-table light handy.

### 1. Open the site
1. Open **Chrome** and go to **ishoponamazonwaytoomuch.ohbah.com**.
2. Log in as **theowner** if asked. You'll see "Welcome back, boss!".

### 2. Put it on your home screen
1. In Chrome, tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the right end of the address bar.
2. Tap **Add to Home Screen**. If you don't see it, scroll the list down a little.
3. Keep the name **Reseller Matrix** (or shorten it), then tap **Add**.
   - Don't see "Add to Home Screen" at all? Your iPhone may need an update (iOS 16.4 or later). Tell us and skip to step 4 in Chrome.

### 3. Open it from the home screen
1. Tap the new red **RM** icon. It opens straight to **Add item**.
2. It may ask you to log in **once more**: the home-screen icon keeps its own login. After that it stays logged in for 90 days.

### 4. Add one real item
1. Tap **Take photo** and take a photo of the item. Allow the camera if your iPhone asks.
2. Tap **Take another** for each extra photo (2 or 3 is plenty), then **Done**.
3. On your best photo, tap **Make cover**. Use the arrows to change the order if you like.
4. Optional: open **Details** and type a title and price.
5. Tap **Save item**. You'll see the item with what it still needs before listing.

### 5. Look it over
- Are the photos the right way up and sharp?
- Did anything look odd, slow, too small to tap, or confusing?

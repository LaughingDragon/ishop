## Instructions

Your listings are all on the site now, but about 70 of them have no photos yet. Adding them is quick: for each item you pick its photos from your phone in one go, and the site files them under that item.

### What to do

1. Open the site's front desk. Under **Needs you now** you'll see "70 items have no photos yet". Tap **Add photos**. (Or, in the **Stockroom**, tap the gold **Add photos** button on any item.)
2. You're on that item's photo page; its name is at the top. Tap **Choose photos** and select all of that item's photos at once in your Photos app (usually 3 to 5, up to 10). Tap **Add**.
3. Wait until they've all uploaded. The first photo is the cover. Use **Make cover** or the arrows to change the order, and **✕** to drop one.
4. Tap **Save and go to the next item without photos**. The next item opens straight away. Repeat.
   - Don't have photos of that one yet? Tap **Skip this item**.
5. Stop whenever you like. The Needs you now line shows how many are left.

Sold items don't need photos. You won't be asked for them.

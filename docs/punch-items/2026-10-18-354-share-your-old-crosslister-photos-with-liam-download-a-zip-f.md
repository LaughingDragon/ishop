## Instructions

Most of your item photos from the old Crosslister app are stored on GitHub, in the private project **SparksFatherOfJarvy/crosslister**. Liam will put them onto your items. All you need to do is download the project as one ZIP file and share it with Liam on Google Drive. This takes about 10 minutes. A computer is easiest: the file is big (around 450 MB).

### What to do

1. Open **https://github.com/SparksFatherOfJarvy/crosslister**. You're already signed in to GitHub, so the project should open. (If it says **404** or "Page not found", you're signed in to a different account: stop and see How to answer.)
2. Tap the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**. A file called **crosslister-main.zip** (or similar) downloads. Wait until it has finished.
3. Open **Google Drive** (drive.google.com) and drag the ZIP file in (or tap **New**, then **File upload**). Wait until the upload says it's complete.
4. In Drive, right-click the file (on a phone: tap the three dots next to it) and choose **Share**.
5. In the box at the top, type Liam's email address: **[email removed]**. Leave **Notify people** ticked and tap **Send**. Google emails Liam the link to the file.

That's all. You don't need to change anything on GitHub. After Liam has it, you can delete the ZIP from your computer and from Drive.

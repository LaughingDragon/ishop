## Instructions

The site can now bring in everything you have listed right now from your spreadsheet (the "Reselling - Master Tracker" file). Nothing on Poshmark, Mercari, Vinted or anywhere else is touched or relisted: the site only reads your file. You see every item before anything is saved, and you can undo the whole import for 7 days. It takes about 15 minutes. A computer is easier than the phone for this one, but either works.

### Before you start: fix one row

Row 37 (Adidas joggers) says SOLD? Yes and Sold for $16, but no platform column says SOLD HERE. In your spreadsheet, type **SOLD HERE** in the column of the site where it sold. If you don't remember, leave it: that one item will be left out and you can add it by hand later.

### What to do

1. Get the file. If it lives in Google Sheets: open it, then **File > Download > Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)**. If you already have the .xlsx, use that.
2. On the site, open **My office**, then tap **Import listings** (https://ishoponamazonwaytoomuch.ohbah.com/room/my-office/import-listings).
3. Choose the file and tap **Read my spreadsheet**.
4. **Columns:** the site has already matched each column (Title, List price, Poshmark, Mercari and so on). It reads the **Items** tab. You shouldn't need to change anything. Tap **Next**.
5. **Categories:** check what each "Type" becomes (for example "Home goods" becomes Home & Kitchen). Change any you don't like, then tap **Next: check everything**.
6. **Check the numbers. This is the important part.** At the top is a table of how many listings are **Posted** on each site. The sheet says about **86 on Poshmark, 85 on Mercari, 75 on Vinted, 15 on Depop, 3 on Grailed and 2 on Facebook**. Open each site's app and look at how many active listings you really have. If a number is far off, **stop here** and tell us which one (see How to answer). Don't import.
7. If the numbers look right, choose what to do with the **sold items**. If they have all been shipped and delivered, choose **"already shipped and delivered"**. If some still need to go out, choose **"still to ship"** and they'll show up in the Shipping room. Leave **Fill in descriptions and details from the old Crosslister records** ticked. Tap **Update the list**.
8. Scroll through the items. Ones with a yellow mark have a note: read it. Untick anything you don't want brought in.
9. Tap **Import the ticked items** and wait for it to finish (about a minute).
10. Open the **Stockroom**: your items are there as Listed (sold ones as Sold). Add photos to them whenever you can.

If something looks wrong afterwards, go back to **Import listings** and tap **Undo** next to the import. It deletes only the items it brought in, for 7 days.

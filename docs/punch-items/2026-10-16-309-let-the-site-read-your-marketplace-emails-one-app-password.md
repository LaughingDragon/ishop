## Instructions

The site can now read your marketplace emails (eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Grailed, Vinted, Facebook Marketplace) and put what needs you on the front desk. It can only **read**: it never sends, deletes, or marks anything in your mailbox. To let it in, it needs its own app password for the business Gmail. This takes about 3 minutes.

### What to do

1. On your phone, open **https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords** and sign in to the **business Gmail** if Google asks (the same account the site sends mail from).
2. Under **App name**, type **iShop inbox** and tap **Create**.
3. Google shows 16 letters in four groups. Copy them (or keep that screen open).
4. On the site, open **Site office > Mail setup** (https://ishoponamazonwaytoomuch.ohbah.com/site-office/mail-setup).
5. Scroll down past "Save and send a test email" to the second part, **Marketplace inbox**. Leave the top part alone.
6. Check that **Gmail address** shows the business Gmail. Paste the 16 letters into **App password for the inbox**, then tap **Save and test the inbox**.
7. You should see **"Saved. The site logged in to … and could read it."** If it says **Not saved**, make a new app password (steps 1 to 3) and try again.

Nothing changes on the front desk yet. Next, the site foreman checks the emails already in your inbox to make sure they're read correctly, and only then switches it on.
